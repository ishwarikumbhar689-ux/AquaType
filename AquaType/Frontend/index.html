      How a dive works

      Type

      Pick a duration and difficulty. Correct characters fill the depth gauge and wrong ones are underlined, never hidden.

      Build energy

      A streak of correct keys makes the sea glow and bubble faster. A mistake resets the streak.

      Restore and discover

      XP grows your reef through five stages and unlocks zones with new creatures for your journal.
